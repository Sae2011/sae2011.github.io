Join For College Girls UM

Whether you’re looking for guidance, sisterhood, or a space to grow into your most confident self, there’s a place for you in FCG UM. Below are the different ways to be involved.

Membership Options

Mentor (Upperclassman)

Mentors are experienced students who want to guide and support newer members. This role helps create a real “big sister” environment on campus.

  • Upperclassman
  • Application required
  • Supports mentees + community building

Mentee (First-Year Student)

Mentees are first-year girls who want support while learning college life and building confidence. You’ll have access to guidance, encouragement, and a community that gets it.

  • First-year students (girls)
  • Application required
  • Priority access to member support + events

General Member

General membership is open to anyone who pays dues and requests membership through Engage. General members can attend events and grow within the organization community.

  • Request membership through Engage
  • Pay dues
  • Access to events (priority after mentees + mentors)
  • Can vote and run for E-Board
